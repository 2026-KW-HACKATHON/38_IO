Ship it

  1. Upload viewer.html, reef-kit.js, reef-data.json and scenes.json to the same folder on any static host.
  2. Print the card at 100%. Measure the QR — it must match the size above, or scale will be wrong.
  3. Scan. The page asks for the camera, then finds the card.
